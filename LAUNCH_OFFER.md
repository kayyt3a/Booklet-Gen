# FolioAI recommended launch offer

Status: prepared for founder approval on 6 August 2026.

This document turns the open pricing and product questions into one conservative
paid-beta offer. It is a recommendation, not evidence that the founder has
approved the prices, tax treatment, or legal wording.

## Customer and promise

FolioAI is sold to parents, guardians, and tutors aged 18 or older. The customer
receives an independently generated, printable practice booklet with a short
lesson, worked examples, questions, and an answer key. It is supplementary
practice, not tutoring, school assessment, diagnosis, or an official test.

The central promise is:

> Personalised practice for this student, ready to print, with answers included.

Do not promise guaranteed academic improvement, a particular NAPLAN result, or
content endorsed by a school or assessment authority.

## Initial products

Launch only products that can run in clean-room mode without third-party source
material:

1. Academic Accelerate Mathematics
2. Academic Accelerate English
3. Independent literacy and numeracy practice for Years 3, 5, 7, and 9

Keep Scholarships and Methods Exam out of the customer menu until each has a
dedicated original-authoring guide and a reviewed sample set. The CLI can remain
available for development.

## Pricing

Keep the existing introductory prices:

- One booklet credit: A$7.90
- Ten booklet credits: A$39.00
- New-account welcome credit: one
- Subscription: none during the paid beta
- Credit expiry: none while the account remains active

The ten-credit pack is A$3.90 per booklet, a 50.6 percent reduction from buying
ten single credits. This gives a parent a workbook-like term purchase while the
single credit remains a low-commitment option.

Display one total Australian-dollar price. Australian consumer pricing must
show the total charge, including taxes and unavoidable fees:

https://business.gov.au/legal/fair-trading/display-prices

Do not claim that GST is included unless the seller is registered for GST. The
website should say that prices are total prices and that GST is included only
when legally applicable.

## Voluntary quality promise

FolioAI should offer this customer remedy in addition to rights that apply under
Australian Consumer Law:

1. A technical generation failure automatically returns all reserved credits.
2. A customer can report a materially incorrect, unsafe, unreadable, or
   substantially off-topic booklet within 14 days of generation.
3. Support first offers a no-charge replacement credit or supervised retry.
4. If the replacement also fails, or the problem is major, support offers a
   refund for the affected paid credit to the original payment method.
5. Change-of-mind refunds are not promised after a credit has been used.
6. Nothing in this promise limits remedies required by Australian Consumer Law.

The ACCC states that consumer guarantees cannot be removed by store terms and
that the remedy depends on the nature and seriousness of the failure:

https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-replace-refund-cancel

## Paid-beta limits

- Invite 10 to 20 known parents or tutors.
- Keep the global daily cap at 120 booklet units or lower.
- Do not buy advertising during the first beta.
- Review every support issue and failed generation manually.
- Keep one monitored support address on every receipt and policy page.
- Remain in Stripe test mode until the full acceptance run passes.
